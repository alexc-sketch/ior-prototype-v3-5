Our Delivery Fleet

Matched to your volume, your access, and your schedule.

IOR operates a national fleet of purpose-built delivery vehicles. Whether you need a rigid tanker for a tight-access civil site or a B-Double for a major mine, we have the right vehicle for the job.

Rigid Tankers

Precision delivery for tight-access sites.

IOR's rigid tankers are purpose-built for civil construction sites, smaller mining operations, and locations where access is restricted. Compact, manoeuvrable, and fully equipped for safe bulk delivery.

Capacity: Up to 20,000L per delivery
Best for: Civil sites, construction depots, smaller farms
Access: Tight-access and restricted-entry sites
Compliance: ADG-compliant, metered delivery, delivery dockets

Semi-Trailers

Standard bulk delivery for regional properties.

IOR's semi-trailer fleet is the backbone of our bulk delivery network. Ideal for regional agricultural properties, medium-scale mining operations, and transport depots requiring regular high-volume supply.

How It Works

From enquiry to delivery — a simple, reliable process.

No surprises. No delays. Just reliable bulk diesel delivered to your site, on schedule.
