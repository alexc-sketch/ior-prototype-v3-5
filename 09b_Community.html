Community & Giving Back

We never forget where we came from.

We believe that we are more than a fuel supplier and seek to be a true member of the communities in which we work.

Watch Our Story Contact Us

Creating Joint Value

We are more than fuel.

As an organisation, we seek to ensure that our growth creates joint value between our business and the community. Our approach to supporting Australian communities is via three pillars: Direct support, Social support, and Economic support.

These aren't just words on a page. They're the commitments we make to every town, every station, and every community that relies on IOR to keep the lights on and the engines running.

How We Give Back

Our three-pillar support model.

Our commitment to regional Australia is structured around three pillars that guide every decision we make as a business.

01

Direct Support

Bringing energy to regional Australia, providing a quality service, managing health and safety risks, and managing our environmental impact. We show up where it matters most.

02

Social Support

Corporate Social Responsibility investment, committing to a Reconciliation Action Plan, and providing all who work with IOR an equal opportunity to succeed.

03

Economic Support

Our local employment program, our buy local program, and our engagement with industry bodies that advocate for progressing agriculture and resources industries in Australia.

Our Partnerships

Supporting the organisations that support Australia.

IOR is proud to partner with organisations that share our commitment to regional Australia and the communities that depend on them.

RACQ LifeFlight

RACQ LifeFlight

IOR is proud to play a role in keeping RACQ LifeFlight's aeromedical helicopters in the air. When every second counts, reliable fuel supply isn't just a service — it's a lifeline for regional Queenslanders.

Heart of Australia

Heart of Australia

IOR supplies the fuel that powers the Heart of Australia buses, bringing specialist cardiac care to regional and remote communities who would otherwise go without. We're proud to help keep these heart buses on the road.
