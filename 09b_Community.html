Our Community Commitment

Creating joint value.

As an organisation, we seek to ensure that our growth creates joint value between our business and the community. Our approach to supporting Australian communities is via three pillars: Direct support, Social support, and Economic support.

We seek to build long-standing relationships and earn the trust of the communities in which we work. This means actively engaging with community groups and giving back through targeted social investments.

Explore Our Three Pillars

Our Three Pillars

Three ways IOR supports regional Australia.

01

Direct Support

Bringing energy to regional Australia, providing a quality service, managing health and safety risks, and managing our environmental impact.

02

Social Support

Corporate Social Responsibility investment, committing to a Reconciliation Action Plan, and providing all who work with IOR an equal opportunity to succeed.

03

Economic Support

Our local employment programme, our buy local programme, and our engagement with industry bodies that advocate for progressing agriculture and resources industries in Australia.

Corporate Sponsorships

Supporting those who support regional Australia.

Our core value of supporting and developing regional, rural and remote Australia is what drives us to be the major sponsor of several not-for-profit and profit-for-purpose organisations.

RACQ LifeFlight Rescue

LifeFlight is one of Australia's largest aeromedical charities providing rapid response medical care to thousands of people each year. Queensland's rural and remote communities rely on the LifeFlight helicopters to bring the hospital to them in their darkest hour.

Heart of Australia

Heart of Australia delivers specialist medical services to regional, rural and remote communities across Queensland. Using customised road trains as specialist medical clinics-on-wheels, they have seen over 5,000 patients across 150,000km and helped save more than 250 lives.
