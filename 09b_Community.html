Community & Giving Back

Fuelling the communities that fuel Australia.

We operate in some of the most remote and hardworking regions of the country. For over 40 years, we have believed that investing in these communities is not just a responsibility, it is a privilege.

Our Philosophy

More than just a fuel supplier.

Our footprint spans the vast expanses of regional and outback Australia. These are not just lines on a map to us; they are the towns where our teams live, work, and raise their families.

When local communities thrive, our industry thrives. That is why IOR actively sponsors health initiatives, grassroots sports, and local events that bring regional Australians together. We are proud to give back to the regions that keep this country moving.

Local Impact

Supporting grassroots communities.

Backing local talent.

Regional sporting clubs are the lifeblood of outback towns. IOR provides sponsorships and grants to local rugby, netball, and cricket clubs to ensure regional talent has the resources to compete and thrive.

Celebrating rural heritage.

We proudly sponsor regional rodeos, campdrafts, and agricultural shows. These events preserve Australia's rural heritage and provide vital economic stimulus to outback townships.

Investing in local ideas.

Through targeted community grants, we help fund local infrastructure upgrades, community events, and educational initiatives that create lasting positive impacts in the areas we operate.
