TERMINAL SPECIFICATIONS

Two terminals. One reliable supply chain.

Both terminals were purpose-built for maximum throughput and minimum downtime.

Lytton Terminal, Port of Brisbane.

The largest diesel-only terminal in Queensland. Strategically located on the south side of the Brisbane River to supply Western Queensland and New South Wales.

  • 110ML capacity provides up to 12 additional days of supply buffer
  • 2km underground pipeline and deep-water berth
  • High-speed gantry fills two trailers simultaneously at 2,400 Lpm
  • Designed for AB triple road train access
Enquire About Lytton Access →

Port Bonython Terminal, South Australia.

The largest diesel storage facility in South Australia. Located 25km north of Whyalla, it is perfectly positioned to supply the mining, agriculture, and transport sectors in SA and the NT.

  • 81ML capacity across three massive 27ML tanks
  • High-speed 2-bay loading gantries
  • Uncongested deep-water port access
  • Designed for heavy vehicle and triple road train access
