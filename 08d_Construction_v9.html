THE REAL COST OF GETTING IT WRONG

Machine queuing is killing your margins.

Every hour of machinery downtime on a civil project has a direct cost. Fuel delays, compliance failures, and poor cost visibility compound quickly across a long-duration project.

Machine Downtime

Waiting for a fuel tanker to navigate a live construction site costs you machine hours. Direct-to-equipment wet-hire refuelling eliminates the queue and keeps every piece of plant running at capacity.

  • Refuelling comes to the machine
  • No site access delays or queuing
  • Maximise productive machine hours

Compliance Risk

Temporary fuel storage on a civil site must meet AS1940 requirements. Non-compliant tanks expose your project to stop-work orders and significant liability. IOR deploys certified frac tanks and self-bunded storage on day one.

  • AS1940-certified frac tanks deployed
  • Full documentation on delivery
  • Zero stop-work risk from day one

Cost Visibility

Without live fuel consumption data, project managers cannot accurately allocate fuel costs to individual machines or cost centres. IOR's HyDip™ FMS and Fuelcharge reporting give you real-time spend visibility across every asset on site.

  • Per-machine fuel cost reporting
  • HyDip™ live tank monitoring
  • Consolidated project billing

HOW IOR SUPPORTS CONSTRUCTION PROJECTS

From site establishment to project completion.

IOR's construction fuel model is built around your project schedule, not our logistics calendar.

Stage 1

Fuel infrastructure in place before groundworks begin.

IOR's project team works with your site manager before mobilisation to deploy frac tanks, establish wet-hire schedules, and confirm bulk delivery access routes. By the time your first machine arrives on site, fuel is ready.

Image Asset Pending: IOR project team meeting with a site manager before mobilisation
Stage 2

Direct-to-equipment refuelling throughout the project.

IOR's wet-hire operators attend your site on a scheduled basis, refuelling every piece of plant directly at the machine. Full dip records and delivery dockets are provided for every fill, giving your project accountant accurate cost data from day one.

Image Asset Pending: IOR wet-hire operator refuelling excavator directly on a civil site
Stage 3

Frac tanks relocated as your project footprint moves.

As your project progresses and the work front moves, IOR relocates frac tanks and adjusts wet-hire schedules to match. There are no additional mobilisation fees for tank moves within the project boundary, and no interruption to supply.

Image Asset Pending: Frac tank being relocated on a civil construction site
Stage 4

Per-machine fuel cost reporting for project accountants.

HyDip™ FMS and Fuelcharge reporting give your project accountant accurate fuel cost data by machine, cost centre, or date range. Monthly consolidated invoicing reduces administration and supports accurate project cost-to-complete forecasting.

Image Asset Pending: Project accountant reviewing HyDip FMS fuel cost report on a laptop
Stage 5

Clean demobilisation with zero loose ends.

At project completion, IOR recovers all frac tanks and storage equipment, provides final fuel reconciliation reports, and closes out the project account. Your site is left clean, compliant, and ready for handover.

Image Asset Pending: IOR team completing demobilisation and tank recovery at a civil project site
