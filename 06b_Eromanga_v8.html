EROMANGA REFINERY

Where the IOR story began.

In 1984, IOR started as a small oil refinery in the remote Queensland town of Eromanga. Forty years later, we are one of Australia’s largest independent fuel distributors. The refinery still runs. The crude oil is still local. The commitment has not changed.

FLAGSHIP PRODUCT

Eromanga UMF: The lowest particulate mining diesel.

Eromanga UMF (Underground Mining Fuel) is a highly specialised diesel engineered for underground environments, where exhaust gas particulates pose a serious health and safety risk. It produces lower exhaust gas particulates than any other diesel currently available on the Australian market.

Lowest Particulates

Engineered to produce the lowest exhaust gas particulates of any diesel fuel in Australia. Critical for underground mining environments where air quality directly impacts worker health and safety.

Locally Produced Crude

Refined from locally produced crude oil, not imported. This is a genuine sovereign supply product with no exposure to international shipping disruption or geopolitical risk.

Unbroken Supply Since 1986

The refinery has been continuously processing crude oil since it was commissioned in 1986. No gaps in production. No supply interruptions. Forty years of unbroken output.

COMMUNITY IMPACT

Fuelling far western Queensland.

Located in one of the most remote regions of Australia, the Eromanga Refinery has been a cornerstone of the local community for four decades. We provide training, employment, and business opportunities in a region where economic activity is scarce.

Almost every person who has worked at this refinery has a connection to the communities of Eromanga, Quilpie, and the Channel Country.

FROM REFINERY TO SITE

Sovereign supply, delivered anywhere.

We do not just refine the fuel, we deliver it. Backed by our own national logistics fleet, we transport bulk diesel and Eromanga UMF directly from our refinery to the most remote mining camps and agricultural operations in Australia.

By controlling both the manufacturing and the logistics, we eliminate third-party delays and secure your supply chain.

  • Direct refinery-to-site distribution
  • Dedicated aviation and diesel tanker fleets
  • Remote off-road delivery capability
