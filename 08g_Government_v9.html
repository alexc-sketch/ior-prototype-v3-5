MITIGATING RISK

Government fuel procurement demands more than a broker.

Public sector operations require supply certainty, audit-ready compliance, and ESG accountability that only owned infrastructure can deliver.

Supply Chain Risk

Broker-dependent suppliers cannot guarantee continuity when global markets tighten. Government and defence operations require a fuel partner with physical Australian infrastructure, not intermediary arrangements.

  • Eromanga Refinery sovereign production
  • Lytton and Port Bonython terminals
  • No brokers, no intermediaries

Compliance Burden

Public sector procurement requires ISO certifications, HSE documentation, and ESG reporting that many fuel suppliers cannot produce. Audit failures create reputational and regulatory risk for government agencies.

  • ISO 9001, 14001, 45001 certified
  • Full HSE documentation on request
  • NGER-compliant emissions reporting

Fleet Visibility

Managing fuel across multiple depots, vehicle fleets, and remote facilities without real-time data creates cost overruns, compliance gaps, and accountability issues for departmental fleet managers.

  • Customer Portal real-time transactions
  • Fuelcharge fleet management app
  • HyDip™ remote tank monitoring

HOW IOR SUPPORTS GOVERNMENT OPERATIONS

From tender engagement to operational delivery.

IOR's government partnership model covers every phase, from initial tender engagement through to operational delivery and ongoing compliance reporting.

Stage 1

Engage procurement specialists.

Work directly with our dedicated government tender team to map your departmental fuel requirements. IOR's procurement specialists have extensive experience supporting state and federal government tenders, providing the documentation, certifications, and pricing structures that public sector procurement requires.

Image Asset Pending: IOR procurement specialist meeting with government representatives
Stage 2

Review compliance and ESG.

Access our full suite of ISO certifications, rigorous HSE frameworks, and comprehensive ESG reporting to satisfy public audit requirements. IOR's compliance documentation is maintained to the highest standard, ensuring your procurement team can complete due diligence with confidence.

Image Asset Pending: IOR compliance documentation or HSE certification imagery
Stage 3

Secure sovereign supply.

IOR's owned delivery fleet and national depot network ensures fuel reaches every government site on schedule. Backed by the Eromanga Refinery and major coastal import terminals, our supply chain does not depend on global market conditions or third-party brokers to maintain continuity.

Stage 4

Manage your fleet from one dashboard.

The IOR Customer Portal gives departmental fleet managers real-time visibility over every transaction across every site. Add or suspend RFID tags instantly, set spend limits by vehicle or operator, and generate tax-compliant audit reports. The Fuelcharge App extends this control to field-based fleet operations.

Stage 5

Report on emissions and sustainability.

IOR's NGER-compliant emissions reporting and ESG documentation give government agencies the data they need to meet departmental sustainability targets and satisfy public reporting obligations. Our reporting tools are available on demand, ensuring your team is always audit-ready.
