Manual fuel management
costs you more than time.

Every manual dip, every missing docket, every end-of-month reconciliation scramble is costing your business money. HyDip™ eliminates the guesswork — replacing paper-based processes with precision hardware and cloud-based software that gives you complete visibility of every litre, 24 hours a day.

A complete fuel ecosystem.

As a whole system or installed as individual components, HyDip™ technology simplifies the way your business manages fuel.

24/7 visibility of fuel levels.

Real-time dip measurements for fuel storage tanks in any location. Remotely check tank levels, verify vendor deliveries, and automate your reordering via the HyDip Cloud platform to prevent costly stockouts.

Know exactly who is using fuel.

Deploy HyDip Fuel Tracking devices to monitor fuel usage by individual vehicle and employee using RFID tags. Spot theft early, reduce fuel loss, and generate reliable data in real-time.

Control access to fuel 24/7.

Highly customisable dispensers allow administrators to activate the bowser on-site or remotely. Built with robust stainless steel technology — reliable in all conditions with no printers or card swipes to take your site offline.

The fuel network manager for unattended sites.

Allow fuel transactions to occur 24/7 with no personnel required. Accept payments via the Fuelcharge app, central touch-screen terminals, or issue PIN-protected tags to authorised local account customers.

Up and running in three steps.

From first enquiry to live dashboard — our team handles everything.

Frequently asked questions
