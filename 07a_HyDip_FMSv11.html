The Challenge

Manual fuel management costs you more than time.

Every manual dip, every missing docket, and every end-of-month reconciliation scramble is costing your business money. Unauthorised usage and undetected leaks go unnoticed until the invoice arrives.

HyDip™ eliminates the blind spots. We replace paper-based processes with precision hardware and cloud-based software that gives you complete visibility of every litre, 24 hours a day.

DEPLOYMENT

Up and running in three steps.

From first enquiry to live dashboard, our digital integration team handles everything.

1
Design your solution
Speak to our digital team to spec the right HyDip hardware for your bulk tanks and fleet size. We will map out sensor placement, communication options, and integration requirements before a single cable is run.
2
HyDip is installed
Our technicians deploy the hardware on-site, whether you need standard 4G/Ethernet connectivity or a remote off-grid solar setup. All hardware is robust and engineered for Australian conditions.
3
Manage your fuel
Log in to the HyDip Cloud dashboard to manage RFID tags, set dispense limits, run reports, and connect your data directly to Xero, MYOB, or SAP. Your team is live from day one.
