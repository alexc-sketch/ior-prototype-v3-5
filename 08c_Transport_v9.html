The Real Cost of Getting It Wrong

We know what slows your fleet down.

In the transport sector, downtime is lost revenue. Before we talk solutions, we need to understand your risks, because IOR's network was built to eliminate exactly these three.

Route Inefficiency

Forcing drivers to detour off major freight corridors to find reliable high-flow diesel burns hours of driving time.

  • 115+ sites on major freight routes
  • High-flow bowsers (130L to 160L/min)
  • Designed for AB-Triple and Quad access

Driver Retention & Amenities

Drivers expect secure, well-equipped facilities. Sites without high-clearance canopies or truck washes lead to frustration and high turnover.

  • Drive-through heavy vehicle truck washes
  • High-clearance canopies
  • 24/7 secure, well-lit facilities

Disconnected Data

Chasing paper dockets and manually reconciling fuel usage across a national fleet wastes days of administrative time and invites fraud.

  • Real-time tracking via Fuelcharge
  • HyDip™ FMS integration
  • Automated Xero/MYOB reporting

Australia's premier transport fuel network.

IOR provides comprehensive fuel solutions for Australia's transport and logistics sector. With a rapidly expanding network of over 115 unmanned, 24/7 diesel stops located on major national freight corridors, we ensure your fleet remains moving efficiently.
