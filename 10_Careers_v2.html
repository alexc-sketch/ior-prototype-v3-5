Careers at IOR

Build a career that keeps Australia moving.

IOR might be the great Aussie success story you haven't heard of yet. From humble roots in Eromanga, we've been fuelling the furthest corners of Australia for over 40 years. And the most exciting journey is still on the horizon.

Why Work With Us?

We're more than fuel, just ask our people.

It's the scale, complexity, and ambition involved in rolling out each new project, whether that's a remote site or a new digital platform. It's the satisfaction of growing market share and expanding rapidly into new industries.

We have a massive pipeline of projects, and we need your help to deliver them. Our goal is to become the premier commercial fuel supplier in the Australian market, and with people like you at the wheel, we know we can get there.

Diversity & Inclusion

From commitment to action.

At IOR, we believe that a diverse workforce makes us stronger, smarter, and better equipped to serve the communities we operate in. We're committed to building an inclusive workplace where every team member can thrive, regardless of background, gender, age, or experience.

We actively support pathways for women in transport, First Nations employment, and regional community hiring. When everyone has a seat at the table, the whole team goes further.

"IOR gave me the opportunity to prove myself in an industry that doesn't always make it easy for women. I've never looked back."

How to Apply

Our recruitment process.

1

Apply Online

Submit your application via SEEK or our careers portal. It takes less than 10 minutes.

2

Phone Screen

A quick call with our recruitment team to learn more about you and the role.

3

Interview

Meet the team in person or via video. We keep it relaxed and genuine.

4

Pre-Employment Checks

Reference checks, background screening, and any role-specific requirements.

5

Welcome to IOR

Onboarding, induction, and your first day with the team. You are officially going far.
