Careers at IOR

Go far
with IOR.

IOR might be the great Aussie success story you haven't heard of — yet. From humble roots in Eromanga, QLD, we've been fuelling the furthest corners of Australia for over 40 years. But we think the most exciting journey's still on the horizon.

View Current Jobs → Explore Entry Pathways →

Why Work With Us?

We are more than fuel, just ask our people.

It's the scale, complexity and ambition involved in rolling out each new project, whether that's a remote, unmanned site or diesel storage facility. It's the satisfaction of growing market share for our emissions-reducing diesel exhaust fluid. And it's the excitement of expanding rapidly into the niche aviation fuel space.

We've got so many projects in the pipeline. And we need your help to deliver. Because our goal is to become the premier commercial fuel supplier in the Australian market. And with people like you at the wheel, we know we can get there.

Our Employee Value Proposition

Three reasons to choose IOR.

01 Right Team

People you trust. Relationships that go the distance.

There's just something about the people at IOR. It's hard to put your finger on, but you feel it right away. It's the kind of place where you walk in the door and instantly know you belong.

02 Right Track

The more we grow, the further you go.

You can feel it from day one. The energy, the growth — the sense that this place is going somewhere. And you're not just along for the ride. You're behind the wheel.

03 Right Now

Big work. Big impact. Get sh*t done.

It's always been the IOR way to chase the opportunities. From day one, we've been a dog with a bone, driven to grow and innovate. Our customers and communities can't wait, so neither do we.

Diversity & Inclusion

From commitment to action.

At IOR, we believe that a diverse workforce makes us stronger, smarter, and better equipped to serve the communities we operate in. We're committed to building an inclusive workplace where every team member can thrive — regardless of background, gender, age, or experience.

We actively support pathways for women in transport, First Nations employment, and regional community hiring. Because when everyone has a seat at the table, the whole team goes further.

"IOR gave me the opportunity to prove myself in an industry that doesn't always make it easy for women. I've never looked back."

Jess Akers, IOR Team Member

How to Apply

Our recruitment process.

01

Apply Online

Submit your application via SEEK or our careers portal. Takes less than 10 minutes.

02

A quick call with our recruitment team to learn more about you and the role.

03

Interview

Meet the team — in person or via video. We keep it relaxed and genuine.

04

Pre-Employment Checks
