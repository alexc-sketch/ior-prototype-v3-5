Careers at IOR

Go far with IOR.

IOR might be the great Aussie success story you haven't heard of yet. From humble roots in Eromanga, we have been fuelling the furthest corners of Australia for over 40 years. We think the most exciting journey is still on the horizon.

View Current Jobs ↗ Explore Entry Pathways

Why Work With Us?

We are more than fuel. Just ask our people.

It is the scale, complexity, and ambition involved in rolling out each new project, whether that is a remote site or a new digital platform. It is the satisfaction of growing market share and expanding rapidly into new industries.

We have a massive pipeline of projects, and we need your help to deliver them. Our goal is to become the premier commercial fuel supplier in the Australian market. With people like you at the wheel, we know we can get there.

Diversity & Inclusion

From commitment to action.

At IOR, we believe that a diverse workforce makes us stronger, smarter, and better equipped to serve the communities we operate in. We are committed to building an inclusive workplace where every team member can thrive, regardless of background, gender, age, or experience.

We actively support pathways for women in transport, First Nations employment, and regional community hiring. When everyone has a seat at the table, the whole team goes further.

"IOR gave me the opportunity to prove myself in an industry that doesn't always make it easy for women. I've never looked back."

Jess Akers, IOR Team Member

Apply Online
