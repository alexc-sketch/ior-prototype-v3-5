THE IOR SUPPLY CHAIN

From import to ignition. How we guarantee supply.

We do not rely on third-party distributors. IOR owns and operates a vertically integrated supply chain, ensuring that when the national network comes under pressure, our customers are protected.

01

Strategic Import Terminals

We secure competitive international supply agreements and import directly into our owned coastal terminals at Lytton (QLD) and Port Bonython (SA), bypassing traditional bottlenecks.

02

Sovereign Manufacturing

IOR owns and operates the Eromanga Refinery, Australia’s only inland refinery. This provides a critical sovereign source of domestic fuel deep in the outback, independent of coastal imports.

03

Owned Transport Fleet

Fuel is moved from our terminals and refinery via our own modern fleet of high-capacity tankers. By controlling the logistics, we guarantee delivery schedules to the most remote sites in the country.

04

115+ Diesel Stops

Our distribution network feeds directly into Australia’s largest unmanned 24/7 diesel network, providing high-flow commercial access for transport and logistics fleets nationwide.

05

JIG-Accredited Aviation

We supply Avgas and Jet A-1 to over 32 regional airports. Our vertically integrated model ensures that critical aviation infrastructure never runs dry.

06

On-Site Infrastructure

For remote mining, civil, and agricultural projects, we deploy AS1940-compliant self-bunded tanks and frac tanks, creating secure localised fuel reserves directly where the equipment operates.

07

HyDip™ Technology

The entire supply chain is monitored in real time by HyDip™. We track tank levels remotely, automate reordering, and provide you with granular data on every drop of fuel consumed.
