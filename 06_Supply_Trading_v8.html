Australia’s fuel supply chain is vulnerable.

Import dependency, limited regional storage, and declining domestic refining capacity leave large-scale operators exposed. IOR was built to change that. You need a fuel partner who owns the supply chain, not a reseller.

Supply & Trading: Common Questions.

The Eromanga Refinery is IOR’s sovereign fuel manufacturing facility located in outback Queensland. Operating since 1984, it produces up to 1,250 barrels per day of high-quality diesel, including the unique Eromanga UMF mining fuel.

IOR operates two strategic import terminals: Lytton Terminal at the Port of Brisbane and Port Bonython Terminal in South Australia. Both feature deep-water berths for bulk fuel imports.

IOR’s two import terminals provide a combined storage capacity of 191 megalitres. Both terminals feature high-speed loading gantries and operate 24 hours a day.
