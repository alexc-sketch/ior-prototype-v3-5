THE IOR SUPPLY CHAIN

From import to ignition. How we guarantee supply.

We do not rely on third-party distributors. IOR owns and operates a vertically integrated supply chain, ensuring that when the national network comes under pressure, our customers are protected.

1
Terminals & Imports
We secure competitive international supply agreements and import directly into our owned coastal terminals at Lytton (QLD) and Port Bonython (SA), bypassing traditional bottlenecks.
2
Refinery
IOR owns and operates the Eromanga Refinery, Australia's only inland refinery. This provides a critical sovereign source of domestic fuel deep in the outback.
3
Bulk Fuel Distribution
Fuel is moved via our own modern fleet of high-capacity tankers. By controlling the logistics, we guarantee delivery schedules to the most remote sites.
4
Fuel Network
Our distribution network feeds directly into Australia's largest unmanned 24/7 diesel network, providing high-flow commercial access nationwide.
5
Aviation Network
We supply Avgas and Jet A-1 to over 32 regional airports. Our vertically integrated model ensures that critical aviation infrastructure never runs dry.
6
Bulk Tanks & Storage
For remote mining, civil, and agricultural projects, we deploy AS1940-compliant self-bunded tanks and frac tanks directly where the equipment operates.
7
HyDip™ Technology
The entire supply chain is monitored in real time by HyDip™. We track tank levels remotely, automate reordering, and provide you with granular data.
