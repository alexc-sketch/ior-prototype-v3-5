The Challenge

Securing Australia's fuel supply chain.

In a market reliant on imported fuel and concentrated coastal storage, large-scale operators require absolute certainty. You need a fuel partner who owns the physical supply chain, not a reseller. From import to ignition, here is how we guarantee supply.

Terminals

We secure competitive international agreements and import directly into our owned coastal terminals at Lytton and Port Bonython, bypassing traditional bottlenecks.

Refinery

IOR owns and operates the Eromanga Refinery, Australia's only inland refinery. This provides a critical domestic fuel source deep in the outback.

Bulk Transport

Fuel is moved via our own modern fleet of high-capacity tankers. By controlling the logistics, we guarantee delivery schedules to the most remote sites.

Diesel Network

Our distribution network feeds directly into Australia's largest unmanned 24/7 diesel network, providing high-flow commercial access nationwide.

Aviation

We supply Avgas and Jet A-1 to over 32 regional airports. Our vertically integrated model ensures that critical aviation infrastructure never runs dry.

On-Site Tanks

For remote projects, we deploy AS1940-compliant self-bunded tanks and frac tanks, creating secure localised fuel reserves where the equipment operates.

HyDip™ FMS

The entire supply chain is monitored in real time by HyDip™. We track tank levels remotely, automate reordering, and provide granular usage data.
