School Based Program

A head start on your future.

IOR's school-based program is designed for Year 10, 11, and 12 students who want to get a head start on their career. You'll work part-time alongside IOR team members, gaining real experience in a professional environment while you complete your studies.

Authentic IOR school based photography pending

What You'll Get

The benefits of joining IOR.

Real Workplace Experience
Gain genuine, hands-on experience in a professional workplace — not just observation.
Flexible Hours
Work hours are designed to fit around your school schedule, with flexibility during exam periods.
Paid Work
Receive a competitive wage for your time, helping you build financial independence while you study.
Pathway to Full-Time
Many school-based participants are offered full-time roles, apprenticeships, or traineeships on completion.
Mentoring & Support
Be supported by an experienced IOR team member who will guide you through your time in the program.
Certificate Qualification
Work towards a nationally recognised certificate qualification as part of your school-based program.

How to Apply

Three simple steps.

01
Express Interest
Contact IOR's HR team or apply through SEEK to express your interest in the school-based program.
02
School Approval
Work with your school's VET coordinator to get approval for the program as part of your senior studies.
03
Start the Program
Begin your school-based placement with a structured induction and meet your IOR mentor.
