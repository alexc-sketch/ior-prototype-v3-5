100% Australian Owned

Family-owned. Fully independent. Built for Australia.

IOR is a 100% Australian-owned and operated fuel business. We don't answer to foreign shareholders — we answer to the communities we serve. From Eromanga to every state, our sovereign supply chain keeps Australia moving.

Founded in 1984 on a simple belief: Australian businesses deserve a fuel partner they can actually rely on. One that shows up, delivers on time, and stands behind every litre.

Our Heritage

A History of Innovation and Growth

1984
IOR is Founded
Founded by John Bonner and Arthur Nommensen as Inland Oil Refinery, operating a small oil refinery in Eromanga.
1996
Leadership Expands
Stewart Morland joins IOR, leading to discovery of Inland Oil Field 7L99. Ross Mackenzie joins as General Manager.
2003
First Unmanned Diesel Stop
IOR establishes the first unmanned 24/7 diesel stop in Winton, QLD — entering the long-haul transport industry.
2014
Aviation Expansion
Aviation fuels added to expand and strengthen services to remote and regional areas.
2025
Stonepeak Acquisition
Stonepeak acquires 75% interest, furthering IOR's position as a critical pillar of essential fuel distribution in Australia.

Transport Capability

Unmatched Transport Capability

With a large fleet of modern trucks, IOR is prepared to go on roads many competitors cannot. We operate approximately 100 trucks and 300 trailers — rigid body, prime movers, B-double, triple and quad combinations. Fully accredited in NHVAS mass, base, fatigue, and maintenance management.
