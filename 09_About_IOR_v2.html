100% Australian Owned

Family-owned. Fully independent. Built for Australia.

IOR is a 100 percent Australian-owned and operated fuel business. We do not answer to foreign shareholders. We answer to the communities we serve. From our inland refinery in Eromanga to every state and territory, our sovereign supply chain keeps Australia moving.

Founded in 1984 on a simple belief: Australian businesses deserve a fuel partner they can actually rely on. One that shows up, delivers on time, and stands behind every litre.

Our Heritage

40 years of fuelling Australia.

1984
IOR is Founded
Founded by John Bonner and Arthur Nommensen as Inland Oil Refinery, operating a small oil refinery in Eromanga.
1996
Leadership Expands
Stewart Morland joins IOR, leading to the discovery of Inland Oil Field 7L99. Ross Mackenzie joins as General Manager.
2003
First Unmanned Diesel Stop
IOR establishes the first unmanned 24/7 diesel stop in Winton, entering the long-haul transport industry.
2014
Aviation Expansion
Aviation fuels are added to expand and strengthen services to remote and regional areas.
2025
Stonepeak Acquisition
Stonepeak acquires a 75 percent interest, furthering IOR's position as a critical pillar of essential fuel distribution in Australia.
