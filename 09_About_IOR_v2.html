Who We Are

We are more than fuel.

IOR is an Australian operated business, dedicated to service and committed to local, hands-on relationships with our customers and suppliers. We are passionate about making sure our hard-working customers across Australia can fuel up, keep going, and keep growing.

We own every part of our supply chain. From our inland refinery in Eromanga to our proprietary HyDip® Fuel Management System, we invest in the infrastructure that keeps Australia moving.

Our Story

40+ years fuelling Australia.

From a single refinery in outback Queensland to a national network of over 115 sites, IOR has been the quiet achiever keeping Australia's hardest-working industries moving.

1984

Eromanga Refinery Founded

IOR begins operations at the Eromanga Refinery in outback Queensland — the foundation of everything that follows.

2005

First Unmanned Site, Winton

IOR opens its first unmanned fuel site in Winton, QLD, pioneering the model that now powers 115+ sites nationally.

2014

Aviation Fuels Added

IOR expands into aviation fuels, beginning a rapid growth trajectory in one of Australia's most demanding supply sectors.

2016

HyDip® FMS Launched

IOR launches the proprietary HyDip® Fuel Management System, giving customers real-time visibility of their fuel assets.

2023

115+ Sites Nationally

IOR reaches a milestone of 115+ unmanned sites across Australia, cementing its position as the nation's leading independent fuel network.

2025

Continued Expansion

IOR continues to grow its network, team, and technology — the most exciting chapter is still ahead.
