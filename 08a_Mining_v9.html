THE REAL COST OF GETTING IT WRONG

We know what keeps mine site managers up at night.

Remote operations have zero margin for fuel failure. Before we talk solutions, we need to understand your risks — because IOR was built to eliminate exactly these three.

⛓️
Supply Chain Vulnerability
A single logistics failure — a flooded road, a supplier stockout, a third-party breakdown — can shut your entire site down. Most fuel suppliers are brokers. They don't own the chain.
IOR owns the supply chain from refinery to site. No middlemen. No single points of failure.
📋
Compliance Exposure
Non-compliant temporary storage is a regulator's first target. AS1940 violations on a remote site don't just attract fines — they trigger shutdowns and reputational damage that outlasts the project.
Every IOR frac tank and self-bunded unit is AS1940 compliant, commissioned and documented before first fill.
📍
Remote Access Limitations
Most fuel suppliers won't go where your mine is. Pilbara, Bowen Basin, Tanami — if it's remote, it's a problem for them. For IOR, it's our specialty.
IOR's owned fleet and regional depot network covers every major Australian mining corridor.
