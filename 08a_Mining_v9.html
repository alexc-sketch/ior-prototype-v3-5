Supply Security

From refinery to remote site. How we guarantee supply.

Supply chain vulnerability is operational risk. We mitigate this by owning the entire journey. We do not rely on third-party brokers or external logistics. Here is how IOR delivers absolute supply security to your site.

Stage 1

191ML of coastal terminal storage.

We import directly into our owned coastal terminals at Lytton (QLD) and Port Bonython (SA). With 191ML of combined storage, we hold the strategic reserves necessary to buffer your operation from global shipping delays.

Image Asset Pending: Aerial view of Lytton Terminal tanks
Stage 2

Australia's only inland refinery.

We own and operate the Eromanga Refinery, located 1,000km west of Brisbane. It provides a critical domestic fuel source and produces Eromanga UMF — the lowest particulate mining diesel on the market.

Image Asset Pending: Eromanga Refinery processing towers
Stage 3

Owned heavy logistics fleet.

Fuel is moved via our own modern fleet of high-capacity tankers and AB-triple road trains. By controlling the transport, we guarantee delivery schedules to the most remote extraction sites without third-party delays.

Image Asset Pending: IOR AB-Triple Road Train driving in outback
Stage 4

AS1940-compliant on-site storage.

We bring the fuel farm to you. We deploy AS1940-compliant self-bunded storage and high-capacity frac tanks directly to your site, creating a secure, localised fuel reserve where your heavy equipment actually operates.

Image Asset Pending: Frac tanks or self-bunded tank deployed on a mine site
Stage 5

Real-time monitoring via HyDip™ FMS.

The entire chain is tied together by HyDip™ FMS. We monitor your on-site tank levels in real time, triggering automated bulk replenishments before you ever run low. This eliminates the safety risks of manual dipping at heights.
