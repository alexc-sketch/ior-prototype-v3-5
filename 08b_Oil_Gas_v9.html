SOVEREIGN SUPPLY CHAIN

From refinery to well site. How we guarantee supply.

Supply chain vulnerability is operational risk. We mitigate this by owning the entire journey. We do not rely on third-party brokers or external logistics. Here is how IOR delivers absolute supply security to your upstream operations.

191ML of coastal terminal storage.

We import directly into our owned coastal terminals at Lytton (QLD) and Port Bonython (SA). With 191ML of combined storage, we hold the strategic reserves necessary to buffer your operation from global shipping delays.

Image Asset Pending: Aerial view of Lytton Terminal tanks

Australia's only inland refinery.

We own and operate the Eromanga Refinery, deep in the Cooper Basin. It provides a critical domestic fuel source and produces Eromanga UMF, the lowest particulate mining diesel on the market.

Image Asset Pending: Eromanga Refinery processing towers

Owned heavy logistics fleet.

Fuel is moved via our own modern fleet of high-capacity tankers and AB-triple road trains. By controlling the transport, we guarantee delivery schedules to the most remote extraction sites without third-party delays.

Image Asset Pending: IOR AB-Triple Road Train driving in outback

Unmanned 24/7 diesel network.

Our distribution network feeds directly into Australia's largest unmanned 24/7 diesel network, providing high-flow commercial access nationwide for your road-going fleets.

Image Asset Pending: IOR unmanned diesel stop

Aviation fuel security.

We supply Avgas and Jet A-1 to over 32 regional airports. Our vertically integrated model ensures that critical aviation infrastructure for fly-in-fly-out (FIFO) workers never runs dry.

Image Asset Pending: IOR aviation fuelling truck

AS1940-compliant on-site storage.

We bring the fuel farm to you. We deploy AS1940-compliant self-bunded storage and high-capacity frac tanks directly to your site, creating a secure, localised fluid reserve where your heavy equipment actually operates.

Image Asset Pending: Frac tanks deployed on an O&G site

Real-time monitoring via HyDip™ FMS.

The entire chain is tied together by HyDip™ FMS. We monitor your on-site tank levels in real time, triggering automated bulk replenishments before you ever run low.

Image Asset Pending: HyDip dashboard monitoring O&G site tanks
