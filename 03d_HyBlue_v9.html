WHAT IS ADBLUE®?

Protect your engines. Meet your emissions obligations.

AdBlue® (also known as Diesel Exhaust Fluid or DEF) is a high-purity aqueous urea solution used in Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) systems to reduce harmful NOx emissions from diesel engines. Modern Euro 5 and Euro 6 diesel vehicles require a continuous and reliable supply of AdBlue® to operate efficiently.

Running out of AdBlue® can trigger engine derates or shutdowns which causes costly downtime. HyBlue™ by IOR ensures your supply is always available in the right format.

  • Required by all Euro 5 and Euro 6 diesel vehicles
  • Engine derate or shutdown if supply runs out
  • Must meet ISO 22241 purity standards to protect SCR systems
  • Consumption typically 3 to 5 percent of diesel usage

QUALITY & COMPLIANCE

ISO 22241 certified. Every batch, every time.

HyBlue™ is manufactured to ISO 22241 specification. This is the international standard for AdBlue® quality. Every batch is tested to ensure the correct urea concentration (32.5%) and purity levels required to protect your SCR systems and maintain warranty compliance.

  • ISO 22241 certified to meet all OEM warranty requirements
  • 32.5 percent urea concentration precisely controlled
  • Batch traceability and certificate of analysis available

FAQs

Frequently asked questions: HyBlue™ AdBlue®.

AdBlue® is the registered trademark for Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) used in SCR systems. HyBlue™ is IOR's branded AdBlue® product, manufactured to ISO 22241 specification. All three terms refer to the same product: a 32.5 percent aqueous urea solution used to reduce NOx emissions from diesel engines.

AdBlue® consumption is typically 3 to 5 percent of diesel consumption, though this varies by engine type and operating load. For example, a vehicle consuming 100L of diesel per day will typically consume up to 5L of AdBlue®. Contact our team for an accurate consumption estimate.

Using substandard or contaminated AdBlue® can cause crystallisation in your SCR system, damage sensors, and void your OEM warranty. HyBlue™ is ISO 22241 certified and batch-tested to ensure correct urea concentration.

Yes. IOR can coordinate diesel and AdBlue® deliveries on the same schedule. We also supply combination diesel and AdBlue® self-bunded tanks for sites that want to manage both products from a single infrastructure footprint.

AdBlue® should be stored in a cool, shaded location away from direct sunlight, ideally between -11°C and +25°C. It must be stored in dedicated containers. IOR can supply dedicated storage tanks and IBCs to ensure correct storage on your site.
