FAQs

Frequently asked questions about HyBlue™ AdBlue®.

  • Yes. HyBlue™ is available at the pump at select IOR Diesel Stops across the network. You can also purchase 10L and 15L packs at IOR Diesel Stops without a trade account. Use the Find a Location tool to check availability at your nearest stop.

  • Typically 12 months when stored correctly — out of direct sunlight, away from extreme temperatures, and in a sealed, uncontaminated container. HyDip™ FMS can monitor your IBC levels and alert you when stock is running low, helping you manage rotation efficiently.
