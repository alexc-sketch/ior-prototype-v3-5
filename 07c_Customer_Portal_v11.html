What's included

Everything you need to manage your fleet.

Four powerful capabilities, included out-of-the-box with every IOR account.

01 — Visibility

Total network visibility.

Track bulk deliveries as they happen. Integrate with HyDip™ to see real-time dips and usage directly from your tank — know exactly what was delivered versus what was pumped.

ASSET — horizontal.png (bleed top-right)
02 — Security

Lock down your fuel.

Suspend lost tags instantly, set strict volume limits per vehicle, and restrict refuelling to specific days or times. Multiple security layers mean only authorised users access your fuel.

Bank-grade encryption Australian data residency
ASSET — Mobile.png (bleed bottom-right)
03 — Reporting

End the end-of-month scramble.

Export ATO-compliant FTC reports in one click, and schedule automated transaction summaries straight to your inbox. Understand consumption by vehicle, driver, or site.

ASSET — UI snippet (dashboard report view)
04 — Finance

Complete financial control.

Access live terminal gate pricing, view all historical invoices, and manage your account balance in real-time. Download tax-compliant invoices instantly and forecast fuel costs with confidence.
