Condensate Supply

Structuring supply agreements that match your operational requirements.

Our trading team works directly with upstream producers and downstream industrial customers to structure supply agreements that deliver security, flexibility, and value.

Direct Producer Relationships

We work directly with upstream producers to secure reliable condensate supply, bypassing intermediaries and ensuring supply chain transparency. Our relationships span Australia's key condensate-producing basins, giving us access to reliable volume at competitive terms.

Bulk Distribution Capability

Leveraging our national terminal network and logistics fleet, we provide bulk condensate distribution to industrial and manufacturing customers across Australia. Our Port Bonython and Lytton terminals provide strategic storage and distribution hubs for condensate movements.

Specialised Trading Expertise

Our Supply & Trading team has deep expertise in condensate trading and supply chain structuring, ensuring your operational requirements are met. We understand the technical specifications and quality requirements of industrial condensate applications.

Why IOR Condensates

Built for security. Designed for flexibility.

IOR's condensate trading operations are designed to provide industrial customers with reliable, flexible supply agreements that mitigate market volatility and ensure operational continuity.

Supply Security

Direct producer relationships and a national terminal network ensure reliable condensate supply, even in volatile markets. We hold strategic inventory positions to buffer against supply disruptions and price volatility.

Flexible Agreements

We structure supply agreements that match your specific volume, delivery, and pricing requirements. Whether you need spot supply, term contracts, or a combination of both, our trading team will structure an arrangement that works for your operation.

Technical Expertise

Our trading team understands the technical specifications and operational requirements of industrial condensate applications. We work with your technical team to ensure product quality and specification compliance across every delivery.
