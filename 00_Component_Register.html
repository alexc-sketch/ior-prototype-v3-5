3. Figma Build Priority

Build these components first. Completing the top 8 will unlock the design of over 80% of all pages on the site.

# Component Used On Why First
1
Global Shell (Nav + Footer)
Desktop + Mobile. Utility bar + main nav + footer columns.
 40 pages Every single page depends on this. Build once, apply everywhere.
2
Hero — Full Bleed Image / Scrim
Full-width, edge-to-edge. Dark scrim overlay. H1 + subtext + CTA pair.
 25 pages The most common hero type. Covers all Ground Fuels, Aviation, Equipment and Supply pages.
3
Hero — Full Bleed Video
Autoplay muted video background. Same overlay + copy structure as image hero.
 16 pages Used on Homepage, key product pages, and Contact Us. Variant of the image hero.
4
Quick Links Bar (EXPLORE)
Horizontal pill links. Active state = gold underline. Never sticky.
 28 pages Appears immediately below the hero on all hub and product pages. Critical for wayfinding.
5
Stat Bar / KPI Strip
3–4 stats in a dark navy band. Large number + short label.
 20 pages Trust signal used on all hub pages and major product pages.
6
Feature Cards (3-col grid)
Icon + heading + body text. White cards on light grey background.
 19 pages The primary content layout component across all product pages.
7
Lead Capture / CTA Form
Navy background band. Heading + subtext + form fields + submit CTA.
 30 pages The primary conversion component. Present on every product and utility page.
8
Pre-footer CTA Band
Full-width gold or navy band. Heading + 1–2 CTA buttons.
 39 pages Present on virtually every page. The last conversion opportunity before the footer.
9
Related Content Grid
3-col card grid. Image + category tag + title + short description + CTA.
 27 pages Used on all product and bio pages to drive internal linking.
10
FAQ Accordion
Expandable rows. Question + answer. Supports schema markup.
 38 pages Present on almost every page. Critical for SEO and E-E-A-T.

4. Designer Notes

Key rules and references for the Figma design team.

What is Locked vs What You Own

The UX structure, copy, and section order are locked in the HTML wireframes. Your role is to own the visual execution — imagery, iconography, typography scale, colour application, and spacing. Do not change copy or remove sections.

Geometry Rules

Rounded corners are permitted and encouraged, particularly on imagery, cards, and icon containers. All hero banners and major section banners must be full-bleed (edge-to-edge). Do not use gradients on hero imagery — use a solid dark scrim overlay instead.

Colour Palette — Core IOR

  • Navy: #0B1F3A — primary backgrounds
  • Gold: #C8A04A — CTAs, accents, active states
  • Mid Blue: #1E3A5F — secondary backgrounds
  • Light Grey: #F4F6F9 — section backgrounds
  • White: #FFFFFF — card backgrounds

Sub-Brand Colour Tokens

Fuelcharge™
--fc-dark: #1A0A2E
--fc-teal: #00C9A7
HyDip™
--hd-navy: #0D2137
--hd-teal: #00B4C8

Quick Links Bar Behaviour

The Quick Links Bar must never be sticky. It scrolls with the page. The active state is a gold underline beneath the current page's link. Each section has its own EXPLORE label (e.g., "EXPLORE Ground Fuels", "EXPLORE Digital Platforms").
