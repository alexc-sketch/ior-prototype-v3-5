Seasonal Surges

Demand for diesel spikes during harvest. Suppliers without a strong regional footprint or guaranteed supply often leave farmers stranded when they need fuel the most.

HOW IOR KEEPS HARVEST RUNNING

From pre-season planning to harvest-day delivery.

IOR's agricultural supply model is built around your season, not our logistics schedule.

Stage 1

Your season starts with a conversation.

IOR's agricultural account managers contact clients before each season to forecast demand, schedule deliveries, and ensure fuel storage is sized correctly. Pre-season planning eliminates the risk of running short during your harvest window.

Image Asset Pending: IOR account manager meeting with a farmer pre-season
Stage 2

Fuel storage commissioned before first fill.

IOR supplies and commissions AS1940-compliant self-bunded storage tanks from 13kL to 100kL. All tanks are installed, documented, and ready before your first bulk delivery. No delays, no compliance risk.

Image Asset Pending: Self-bunded storage tank being installed at an agricultural property
Stage 3

Bulk diesel to your farm gate.

IOR's owned delivery fleet delivers bulk diesel directly to your farm gate on a scheduled or on-demand basis. Our rural delivery network covers all major Australian grain belts: Darling Downs, Riverina, Eyre Peninsula, and beyond.

Image Asset Pending: IOR tanker delivering to a farm gate in a grain belt region
Stage 4

Fleet fuel managed from your phone.

The Fuelcharge App gives you real-time visibility over every litre your fleet consumes. Set limits by vehicle or operator, track spend across your operation, and access 115+ IOR diesel stops nationwide, all from a single dashboard.

Image Asset Pending: Fuelcharge App dashboard on a mobile device
Stage 5

Emergency response during harvest.

When unexpected demand spikes during harvest, IOR's emergency delivery capability means your operation does not stop. Our owned fleet and 115+ stop network gives us the flexibility to respond faster than any broker-dependent supplier.

Image Asset Pending: IOR tanker responding to an emergency agricultural delivery
