Occupational Health & Safety

A culture of safety first.

IOR is committed to providing a workplace that is safe for both our team members and our customers. Through a consultative approach, we continuously develop and improve safe operating procedures that are practical and effective in the field.

Every team member receives regular training to empower them to identify and minimise risks. We do not view safety as a compliance checklist; it is the baseline requirement for every task we perform.

Environmental Management

Protecting the landscapes we work in.

Operating across regional and outback Australia means we work in some of the most pristine and delicate environments in the world. We take our responsibility to protect these landscapes seriously.

Under our ISO 14001 certification, IOR actively seeks to minimise the environmental impact of our operations. We work collaboratively with local communities to understand the resources they rely upon, ensuring we provide high-quality products responsibly and sustainably.
