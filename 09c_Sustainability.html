Health & Safety

A Culture of Safety First.

IOR is committed to providing a workplace that is safe for both team members and customers. All team members at IOR are responsible for ensuring health and safety is managed throughout the company.

Through a consultative approach, IOR continues to develop and improve safe operating procedures that are practical. Team members receive regular training to help them champion identifying and minimising risks in their day-to-day duties at IOR.

IOR has third party certification through independent registration body QAS International in the following standards:

  • ISO 45001:2018 & AS/NZS 4801:2001 — Occupational Health and Safety Management Systems
  • PAS 99:2012 — Integrated Management Systems (ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001:2018 & AS/NZS 4801:2001)

Environmental Management

Responsible Operations.

IOR holds third-party certification from QAS International for ISO 14001:2015 — Environmental Management. Under this certification, IOR seeks to minimise the risk of adversely impacting the environment.

This is achieved by consulting and working collaboratively with the communities in which the company operates. IOR team members seek to understand the environmental resources that these communities rely upon so that reasonable steps can be taken to provide high-quality products and services responsibly.

Integrated Management System

Our certifications in detail.

IOR holds third-party certification through QAS International across four integrated management system standards, covering safety, environment, quality, and operational excellence.

ISO 45001:2018 is the international standard for occupational health and safety management systems. It provides a framework to improve employee safety, reduce workplace risks, and create better, safer working conditions.

  • Systematic approach to managing occupational health and safety risks
  • Framework for continual improvement of OH&S performance
  • Applicable to all IOR operations across Australia
  • Third-party certified by QAS International

AS/NZS 4801:2001 is the Australian and New Zealand standard for occupational health and safety management systems. IOR holds dual certification with both ISO 45001:2018 and AS/NZS 4801:2001, demonstrating our commitment to meeting both international and local safety standards.

  • Australian and New Zealand occupational health and safety standard
  • Dual certification with ISO 45001:2018
  • Demonstrates compliance with local regulatory requirements

ISO 14001:2015 is the international standard for environmental management systems. IOR holds third-party certification from QAS International for this standard, demonstrating our commitment to minimising environmental impact across all operations.

  • Framework for managing environmental responsibilities
  • Systematic approach to minimising environmental impact
  • Supports compliance with environmental legislation
  • Drives continual improvement in environmental performance

PAS 99:2012 is the specification for common management system requirements as a framework for integration. IOR's PAS 99 certification covers the integration of ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001:2018, and AS/NZS 4801:2001 into a single, cohesive management system.

  • Integrates ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management)
  • Integrates ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental Management)
  • Integrates ISO 45001:2018 & AS/NZS 4801:2001 (OH&S)
  • Single integrated management system across all IOR operations
