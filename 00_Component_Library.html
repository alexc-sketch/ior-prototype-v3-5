Heroes

Full-bleed hero sections that open every page. All banners must be edge-to-edge with no horizontal padding. The video variant is used on the Homepage only; all other pages use a full-bleed image with a dark scrim overlay.

Full-bleed hero with dark scrim overlay, H1, subtext, and 1–2 CTAs. Used on all product, service, hub, and utility pages. Image must be edge-to-edge (no padding). No gradients.
